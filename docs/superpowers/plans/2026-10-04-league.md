# League ("El Club del Mulligan"): a season-long monthly card league

**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Status:** Plan. Designs approved (canvas, page "v4 A · Fits in"); not started.
**Designs:** https://claude.ai/artifact/LfPgVSS7MmSV7tacMGkieH, page **v4 A**. Option A was chosen, with these changes: the switch on the normal setup, cards played without the app, the existing scorecard grid for confirmation, and no 48 h review.
**Adds:** leagues, league members and handicaps, league cards, marker tokens, league notifications (push and email), the League screens, a public marker page at `/m/<token>`.
**Keeps:** the scorecard and the cards engine, Setup, Feed, the live views, and the Notifications screen. League rounds are ordinary games with one flag.

## 1. Required behaviour

The group: 7 friends ("El Club del Mulligan · Tour 2026"). Each plays one 18-hole card per month, anywhere, usually with a stranger marking them.

| # | Situation | Required |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Marcos creates a league. | Name and season, then rules (one card a month, net Stableford, points table, entry fee), then members with a league handicap each, then an invite link and QR. Created from the Play tab's Tournament tile, as a new "League" kind. |
| L2 | A friend opens the invite link. | They see the league, the rules in brief and the league handicap the admin proposed (they can propose another), then Join. |
| L3 | Marcos plays in the app with Javi. | Normal Setup. On the review step a switch, **"Counts for El Club del Mulligan"**, is shown and can be turned on. When it's on, Start announces the card on the server before any score exists, uses Marcos's league handicap for strokes, and notifies the league. |
| L4 | Javi scores Marcos's card in the same game. | At Finish, if every hole of Marcos's card is settled and was marked by another signed-in player, the card is **confirmed by partner** with no further step. |
| L5 | Marcos's marker doesn't use the app. | After Finish, Marcos shows a QR. The marker scans it with the phone camera, sees the card in the existing scorecard grid on a web page with no login, types their name and taps Confirm. The card is confirmed. "Something's wrong" sends the card back to Marcos with the marker's note. |
| L6 | Pablo plays without the app. | Before playing he **announces** the card (course, tee time) from the League screen; the league is notified and no scorecard starts. After the round he types the 18 holes into the existing scorecard grid and attaches a photo of the signed card (or an official tournament result). Submitting confirms it. |
| L7 | Someone forgot to announce in the app (e.g. announced on WhatsApp). | They can still add the card. It is labelled **"Not announced in the app"** for the group to see. |
| L8 | A member already has a card this month. | A second announcement is refused: "You already have your October card." |
| L9 | The month ends. | Positions are set by **net differential** among confirmed cards (decision 2026-10-05, replacing Stableford points): adjusted gross (net double bogey per hole) → differential = (113 / slope) × (adjusted gross − course rating) → net = differential − league handicap, to one decimal, lowest first. A card needs a rated tee (slope and course rating); the server computes and stores `net_differential` (migration `20261005000001_league_net_differential.sql`). Stableford points stay as secondary info. Season points follow the table 500/300/190/135/110/90/75/60/45/35/25/15, no card scores 0, and **ties share** the summed points equally (two tied 2nd → (300+190)/2 = 245 each). Members are notified that the ranking was updated. |
| L10 | Leaderboard. | Season standings (deep-green leaderboard, as today's tournament board), this month's card statuses, and a line about the December Final and pot. An empty state is shown before the first card. |
| L11 | The group wants to lower someone's handicap. | Any member opens a vote ("Álvaro 30.0 → 27.0"). It passes at the threshold (§10, D2) and the change is recorded in that member's handicap history. |
| L12 | Notifications. | Push and email per event, switched per member: card announced, teed off (first score tap of a league round), card confirmed, ranking updated, 3 days left without a card, handicap vote opened. |

Removed by decision (2026-10-03/04): the mulligan, the marker requirement and the review window are **not** rules or settings. There is **no 48 h review**: a card counts as soon as it is confirmed (partner, QR marker, photo, or official result).

## 2. Model

```
league           one season of one group: rules + points table + invite code
league_member    user in a league: league handicap, fee paid, role (admin | member)
league_card      a member's card for one month: how it was played, how it was confirmed, its frozen score
marker_token     one-time, short-lived token that lets an outside marker see and confirm one card
```

- **A league card points at a game, or holds its own holes.** App cards reference `(tournament_id, round_id, player_id)` and copy the settled strokes when submitted. Off-app cards hold the strokes the member typed. Either way, `league_card.holes` is the frozen snapshot the standings use.
- **Points are computed once, when the card is submitted.** They use the existing `scoring.js` maths with the member's league handicap and the course tee, and are stored on the card with the snapshot. Standings never re-read games, so editing a finished game later doesn't move the league. Re-submitting is admin-only (§5).
- **Announced before the first shot.** `announced_at` is a server timestamp (`now()` in the RPC). For app cards, `first_shot_at` is the round's `body.startedAt` (stamped on the first score tap, PR #91). A card whose `first_shot_at < announced_at`, or that has no announcement, is stored with `not_announced = true`. It is shown, never blocked.
- **Month.** A card belongs to the calendar month of `played_on`, in Europe/Madrid. One live card per member per month (unique index excluding `void`).
- **Standings are a pure function of the cards** (`src/store/leagueStandings.js`). Seven members times twelve months is tiny, so the client computes everything. The month-close job (§6) calls the same rule in SQL only to know who to notify.

`tournaments` gets **no new column**. The game is linked from the league card side (`league_cards.tournament_id/round_id`), so `create_game_tournament` is untouched. The Setup switch also writes `props.league = {leagueId, cardId}` so the scorecard knows it is a league round without another fetch.

### 3.2 RLS and helpers

- `is_league_member(lid, uid)` and `is_league_admin(lid, uid)` are SECURITY DEFINER STABLE helpers, like `is_tournament_member`. EXECUTE is revoked from anon.
- Members can SELECT everything in their league: leagues, members, cards, events, votes, ballots.
- **All writes go through RPCs.** There are no direct INSERT/UPDATE policies on league tables. This keeps the announce timestamp, the one-card rule and points server-side.
- Proof photos go in a new storage bucket, `league-proofs`. It is **private**: league members can read; the card owner writes `<league_id>/<card_id>.jpg`.

| `join_league(p_code, p_proposed_handicap)` | signed in | Adds or activates the membership. If the proposed handicap differs, records a `proposed` event for the admin. |
| `set_league_handicap(p_league, p_user, p_value)` | admin | Capped at `handicap_cap`; writes a `set` event. |
| `open_handicap_vote` / `cast_handicap_ballot` | member | Applies the change when the vote passes (D2), with a `vote` event. |

  - `league_ranking_updated`
  - `league_month_reminder`

- Rows are inserted by the RPCs, and by the scheduled jobs in §6 for the ranking update and reminder. Each event fans out to every other active member.
- **Push:** add renderers in `supabase/functions/send-push/index.ts`, map them in `CATEGORY_BY_TYPE` to a new category `league`, and add the same types to `src/lib/notificationContent.js` (title, icon, deep link to the League screen).
- **Preferences:** today's settings have three category switches (`profiles.settings.notifications.{scores,invites,media}`). Add `league: {push: {<type>: bool}, email: {<type>: bool}}`. The League settings screen edits it. `send-push` and `send-email` read it.

### 3.5 Email (new — the repo sends no email today)

- A new edge function, `send-email`, triggered by the same Database Webhook on `notifications` INSERT, filtered to `league_*` types.
- It renders a small HTML template plus a plain-text alternative per type. The tee-off email is the designed one: course, marker, "Follow live" (opens the existing live view), "This month so far", and "Manage league emails".
- The provider is decision D3. Secrets go only in Supabase function secrets, never in git. Recipient addresses come from `auth.users.email` via the service role. Every email has an unsubscribe link that turns that type's email preference off.

## 5. Edge rules

- **Editing after submit:** the snapshot is frozen. If the member edits the game afterwards, the league card does not change. Fixing it takes `void_league_card` (admin) and a new card.
- **Marker tokens:** single use and 2 h. A new token voids the previous one. `snapshot_hash` blocks confirming a card that changed after the QR was shown.
- **Partner check** uses the server's settled cells (`game_scores`) and `scorer_cards`. A partner who scored the whole group counts, which is what the group wants. A partner signed in with the same account as the owner never counts.
- **Leaving:** a member who leaves keeps their past cards in the standings, marked "left".
- **Handicap cap:** every write is clamped to `handicap_cap`.

## 6. Scheduled jobs

There is no `pg_cron` in the project today. One Supabase Scheduled Edge Function, `league-cron`, runs daily at 09:00 Europe/Madrid:
- **On the 1st:** closes the previous month. It computes positions (SQL mirror of `monthResults`), inserts `league_ranking_updated` for every member, and marks still-`announced`/`playing` cards per D1.
- **3 days before month end:** sends `league_month_reminder` to members with no card.
- **Every run:** closes expired handicap votes (`failed` if the threshold isn't met).

The schedule is cloud configuration, so it needs plan mode and its own PR (§9 P11).

## 10. Decisions to confirm

| # | Question | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | A card was announced but never submitted by month end. | It counts as **no card (0 points)**. The rule says the declared card counts whatever happens, but there is no score to rank. |
| D2 | Handicap vote threshold. | **⌈2/3 of active members⌉** (5 of 7, as in the design). The vote closes after 7 days. |
| D3 | Email provider. | **Decided 2026-10-04: not AGDS, free.** Supabase's built-in SMTP sends only auth emails, is heavily rate-limited, and only reaches project team addresses, so it can't send league email. Use a free transactional tier called from `send-email`: **Resend** (free tier, needs a domain we own and its DNS records) or **Brevo** (free daily allowance, can send from a single verified address without a domain). Seven members send a handful of emails a day, well inside either. To choose before P10: with a domain → Resend; without one → Brevo. |
| D4 | Who can be an in-app partner. | Any signed-in Golf Partner player in the same game, in the league or not (as designed: "Lucía"). |
| D5 | Month boundary timezone. | Europe/Madrid. |
| D6 | The December Final. | **Decided 2026-10-04:** a normal Stableford tournament. Depending on the final standings, players get **extra strokes** (or none). The amounts are set by the admin on the day. See P12. |
| D7 | Fees and pot. | Tracking only: the admin toggles "paid"; pot = paid × fee. No payments in the app. |

D1, D2, D4, D5 and D7 were accepted as proposed on 2026-10-04 (no objection when presented).

## 11. Out of scope

- Payments and prize payouts. The Final's extra strokes are entered by the admin (P12), not calculated by a formula.
- Rendering the QR from offline data: the card must be synced before the QR is shown. Follow-up if courses without signal make this a problem.
- More than one league per group season UI polish (multiple leagues work in the model; the Play tab lists them).
- Dark-mode design pass for the new screens (they use theme tokens, so dark mode works but hasn't been reviewed).
