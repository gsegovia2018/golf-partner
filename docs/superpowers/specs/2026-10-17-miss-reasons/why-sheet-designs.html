1 · Everything on one sheet

The tracer is the hero: tap the band and the flight draws itself with its name. Contact and situation chips sit below. One glance, any order, Done when you're done.

The most "see it all" option. Tallest sheet, at about two-thirds of the screen, and 12 chips is a lot to scan on a sunny tee.

2 · Three quick steps

One question per screen: Where did it start? (the tracer) → How did it feel? → Anything unusual?. Big icon tiles, auto-advance on tap, any step skippable. Shown here: step 2.

Best with gloves and in sun, with fewer things on screen at once. A full answer is 3 taps with motion between them, and you can't see the whole story at once.

3 · Tell it as a sentence

The sheet reads like you'd tell your partner: "It started right, felt heavy, from a normal lie." Tap a highlighted word and its options open below. A small tracer thumbnail sits beside it.

The shortest sheet and the most human. Translates naturally ("Salió a la derecha, pesada, desde un lie normal"). Each slot is two taps (open it, pick), and the tracer is small.

My lean

1 for the first version: the tracer is the payoff you picked two-taps for, and seeing everything at once makes a review easy. If chip overload is a worry, the fix is to show only the 4 most-used situation chips plus "More", rather than splitting it into steps.
