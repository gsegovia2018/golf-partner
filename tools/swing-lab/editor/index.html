How to mark
    Hands Clubhead Estimated Ball Shaft plane Shoulder plane Auto (ghost)

    Scrubbing only previews a frame. Press the button to move the selected checkpoint there. Keys: , / . step 1 frame, with Shift 10.

    Drag a mark, or tap the image to place the selected one. With the image focused, arrow keys move the mark 1 px (Shift: 5 px; Alt + arrows changes checkpoint). Otherwise ←/→ change checkpoint. Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes, Cmd/Ctrl+S saves.

    Recomputes as you drag
